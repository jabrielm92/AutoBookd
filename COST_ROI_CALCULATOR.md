## Hidden Value: Leads Without Emails

**60% of scraped leads won't have emails found.** But you still get:

✅ **Business Name**
✅ **Phone Number**
✅ **Address**
✅ **Website**
✅ **Rating & Reviews**
✅ **Category/Industry**

### What You Can Do:
1. **Call them directly** - Phone numbers are included
2. **Send mailers** - You have their address
3. **Connect on LinkedIn** - Search by company name
4. **Visit in person** - For local service businesses
5. **Send emails manually** - Find emails on their website yourself

**These leads cost you almost nothing** (only the SerpAPI discovery cost).
