Let me walk you through exactly how AutoBookd works, because this might change how you do lead gen forever.

**THE PROBLEM:**

❌ 2 hours searching Google Maps

❌ 20 minutes writing each "personalized" email
❌ Forgetting follow-ups
❌ No system for handling replies

That's 4-5 hours of work for maybe 10-20 outreach emails.

**THE SOLUTION:**

AutoBookd automates the entire flow:

**Step 1: Target Selection**
You tell the system what you want: "HVAC companies in Phoenix" or "Accountants in NYC" - literally any industry in any city.

**Step 2: Lead Discovery**
AI scrapes Google Maps in real-time. Not outdated databases - fresh data including business name, address, phone number, website, Google rating, review count.

**Step 3: Contact Enrichment**
System finds verified email addresses AND phone numbers. Multiple data sources (Hunter.io, Apollo) to maximize hit rate.

**Step 4: AI Research**
Here's where it gets interesting. The AI actually visits each lead's website. Reads their services. Understands their business. Identifies specific pain points. Finds personalization hooks.

**Step 5: Personalized Email Generation**
GPT-4o writes completely unique emails for each lead. Not mail merge templates - actual personalized messages that reference their specific business, services, and challenges.

Example:
> "Hey Mike, noticed you're doing a lot of commercial roofing in the Scottsdale area. With the monsoon season damage this year, I imagine warranty claim calls are through the roof (pun intended). We help contractors like Valley Roofing streamline their estimate process..."

That's AI-written. For each lead. Automatically.

**Step 6: Multi-Email Sequences**
One email isn't enough. AutoBookd creates 4-email sequences with strategic timing:
- Email 1: Personal opener
- Email 2: Pattern interrupt (day 2-3)
- Email 3: Proof/case study (day 5-6)
- Email 4: Breakup (day 10)

**Step 7: Auto or Manual Send**
You choose:
- Turn ON auto-send → Emails go out automatically
- Turn OFF → Emails become drafts you can review, edit, and send manually

**Step 8: Reply Handling**
AI reads incoming replies. Classifies intent (interested, not interested, question). Automatically offers your Calendly link to interested prospects.
